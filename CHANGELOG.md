# v1.0.0
2026-10-29

### New
- Turns the reagents Profession Shopping List is tracking into a TradeSkillMaster search string of exact item IDs, on a PSL to TSM tab in the Auction House or in a window anywhere (`/psltsm convert`).
- Searches for only what you're still missing, skipping reagents you have enough of and soulbound or quest items. Can be switched to the full amount in settings.
- Updates on its own when your tracked recipes or bags change, and splits long strings into parts.
